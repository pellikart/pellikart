# Pellikart — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]. Publish this at a public URL (e.g. https://pellikart.com/privacy)
and enter that URL in App Store Connect and Google Play Console._

Pellikart ("we", "us") connects couples planning a wedding with vendors. This
policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## Information we collect

**Couples**
- Name(s) and phone number (used to sign in by one-time code).
- Optional email.
- Location: your approximate or precise location when you tap "use current
  location", used to show and rank nearby venues/vendors. You can decline; the
  app still works, without distance-based sorting.
- Event details you enter: events, dates, guest counts, budget, and your
  shortlists, selections and bookings.

**Vendors**
- Business profile: name, category, base location, phone, email, Instagram,
  description, experience, team size, and listing details/photos.
- For automatic payouts: bank account details and KYC information (e.g. PAN),
  which we pass to our payment processor to create your payout account. We store
  only your payout account status and the last four digits of your bank account.

**Everyone**
- A device push token (if you enable notifications).
- Basic diagnostics and crash reports (see Third parties → Sentry).

## How we use it

- To run the matching algorithm (location, budget, guest count, dates) and show
  relevant vendors.
- To process the 10% booking amount and, for vendors, to settle payouts.
- To send booking, milestone and visit-request notifications.
- To sign you in and secure your account.
- To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Third parties (processors)

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, authentication, storage, backend functions |
| Razorpay | Payment processing and vendor payouts (incl. KYC) |
| MSG91 | Delivering login one-time codes by WhatsApp/SMS |
| Expo | Push notification delivery |
| Sentry | Crash and error diagnostics |
| Google Maps | Map display and location features |

Each processes data on our behalf under their own terms and security.

## Location

Location is used only to show and sort nearby vendors and to display a venue's
distance from you. Precise location is used only when you explicitly request it,
and is not shared with vendors.

## Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can **delete your account at
any time in the app** (Home / Profile → Delete account), which permanently
removes your account and associated data. Some records may persist briefly in
backups and with processors as required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention
reasons.

## Security

Access is protected by row-level security and authentication. Payment card and
bank details are handled by Razorpay; we never store full card or bank numbers.

## Children

Pellikart is not directed to children under 18.

## Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new
date.
